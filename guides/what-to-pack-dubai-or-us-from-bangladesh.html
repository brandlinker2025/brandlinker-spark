Dubai and the US are different at the socket

Dubai and the rest of the UAE use the UK-style Type G plug, at about 220–240V. Bangladesh is also about 220V, so a charger marked 100–240V usually works once the plug fits.

The United States uses Type A and Type B plugs at about 120V. A travel adapter changes the plug shape. It does not change the voltage. Read the label on each charger. If it says 100–240V, the US plug is enough. A device made only for 220V is not safe on a US socket with an adapter alone.

The universal adapter in the adapter guide includes US, EU, UK and AU plugs. The UK plug is the one for Dubai. The US plug is the one for the United States. The seller says it does not fit Indian Type D sockets, and it does not convert voltage.
