Carry-on packing list

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Pack the cabin bag as if the checked suitcase might not arrive with you. Airline size and weight limits differ by fare, so confirm them on the airline's site before you close the bag.

In the cabin bag

  1. Passport, visa and tickets in a holder you can take out at the desk.
  2. A change of clothes and a wash kit in cubes, with liquids inside the airport limit.
  3. An empty collapsible bottle. Fill it after security.
  4. Neck pillow and sleep mask for the flight.
  5. Power bank and cables. The power bank stays in this bag even if you check another suitcase.
  6. The travel adapter, so you can charge on arrival without opening a checked bag.
  7. Any medicine you need during the flight.

The bag itself

A 20-inch spinner is a common carry-on. A 40L backpack is only a safe personal item if your fare's rules allow that size. Weigh the bag at home.

Leave these decisions to the airline

  • Power banks and spare lithium batteries are cabin-only.
  • Sharp items and larger liquids belong in checked luggage when the airline says so.
  • If you do check a bag, a TSA lock and a tracker are for that bag, not a substitute for the cabin list above.
