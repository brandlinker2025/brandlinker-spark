Returns & Refunds

Returns and refunds are handled case by case. We do not offer a standard “change of mind” or fixed-day returns period.

Damaged or wrong items

If an item arrives damaged or is the wrong item, please contact us at inforsfusionbd@gmail.com with your order ID and clear photos. We will review the case and tell you what we can do.

Supplier policies

If a verified supplier policy applies to that item, we will follow that policy. Otherwise we will review the request case by case. We will not invent a returns window or a guarantee that is not in place.

Refunds

If a refund is agreed, we will use the same payment method where we can, or another method we agree with you. No payment is taken on this website, so a refund only applies after a payment has actually been made.
