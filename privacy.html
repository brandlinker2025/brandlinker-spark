Privacy Policy

This page explains what personal data BrandLinker Spark collects, why we collect it, and where it is stored. BrandLinker Spark is a venture of BrandLinker Headquarters.

What we collect

When you place an order or a pre-order request, we collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number and country
  • Your shipping address (required for paid orders; optional on a pre-order request)
  • Your payment method, when one is chosen (Cash on Delivery, bKash or Nagad). International pre-order requests are saved with no payment method.
  • An optional order note
  • The items you requested, including the variant and quantity
  • Promo code, totals and currency, when a price has been set
  • The time the request was saved, and whether it is an order or a pre-order inquiry

We also store a hashed version of your IP address for basic anti-spam and rate limiting. We do not use it for marketing.

The shopping cart and currency preference are saved only in your browser (local storage).

Why we collect it

We use this information to save your request, contact you to confirm price, payment and delivery, email available payment options for international pre-order requests, and notify the store that a request has arrived.

Where it is stored

Each saved request is stored in a private Vercel Blob store used by BrandLinker Spark. A notification copy of the same details is emailed to inforsfusionbd@gmail.com.

Access keys stay on the server. They are not placed in the public website code.

What we do not collect on this site

We do not collect card numbers, Apple Pay or Google Pay details, or bKash or Nagad wallet numbers on this website.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We may share what is needed with a supplier or payment provider only after we have confirmed the request with you, so that the item can be fulfilled or paid for.

Affiliate links

Some pages include Amazon affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. If you follow one of those links and buy something, Amazon may collect information about that visit according to Amazon’s own privacy policy. BrandLinker Spark does not receive your Amazon account or payment details.

Your requests

To ask about the data we hold, or to ask us to correct or delete it, email inforsfusionbd@gmail.com.
