Shipping

When you place an order or a pre-order request, we will contact you to confirm whether we can deliver to your address, what the shipping cost will be, and an estimated delivery time.

Delivery times

Any delivery time we later share is an estimate only. Times vary by supplier and by country, and they are not guaranteed until we confirm them with you for that order.

Where we can send orders

We will tell you whether we can deliver to your country when we confirm your request. We do not treat a listed country as a guarantee of delivery.

Cost

Shipping costs are confirmed when we contact you. They are not included in a placeholder product price, and we do not offer a free worldwide shipping threshold.
