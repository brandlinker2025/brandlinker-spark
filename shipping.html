Shipping

No payment is taken on this site. When you place an order or a pre-order request, we email you about payment, and we confirm delivery where it is not already stated on the product.

Travel items with a published price

For selected travel items that show a price, shipping to the United States and the United Kingdom is free. Delivery is usually 5-12 business days. That timing is an estimate, not a guaranteed delivery date.

UAE shipping coming soon. You can send a pre-order request. We will email you when we can confirm delivery to the United Arab Emirates.

Shipping to other countries, including Bangladesh, is not confirmed yet for these travel items. You can still send a request, and we will email you about delivery. The request will include the item price and your destination country.

Other store items

For lifestyle pieces and for travel items marked “Price coming soon,” delivery time and any shipping cost are confirmed when we contact you. Product pages for those items show: Delivery time confirmed at checkout.

What we do not promise

We do not promise express, next-day or worldwide shipping, and we do not offer a free worldwide shipping threshold. A country listed in the checkout form is not by itself a guarantee of delivery, except where a product already states free shipping to the United States or the United Kingdom.
