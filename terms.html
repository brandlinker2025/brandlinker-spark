Orders and pre-order requests

Submitting a form on this site is a request, not a completed sale. We will contact you to confirm whether we can supply the item, the price, payment and delivery. Either of us may decide not to go ahead before that confirmation.

Travel Collection items are listed with a placeholder price (“Price coming soon”). A pre-order request for those items is saved as an inquiry. The price is confirmed by email before any payment is due.

Prices and currency

Where a price is shown, you can view it in US dollars (USD) or Bangladeshi taka (BDT). BDT amounts use a fixed site rate of 1 USD = 124.75 BDT, based on the Bangladesh Bank weighted average published on 19 November 2026. This is not a live bank rate and may differ from the rate a bank charges. The rate is also recorded in our product data file.

We may need to change a price before we confirm an order, for example if a supplier cost or shipping cost has changed. We will not take payment until you have agreed the confirmed amount.

Payment

No payment is taken on this website. For orders from Bangladesh we offer Cash on Delivery, bKash and Nagad. For bKash and Nagad we will contact you with payment details. For other countries, checkout is a pre-order request with no payment step: we will email you available payment options to confirm your order. We do not promise a specific international payment method on this site.
